## Overview

This app is a smart voice-to-text transcriber designed to make capturing spoken words as easy and seamless as possible. Imagine you have an idea, need to take notes, or want to transcribe a conversation—just speak, and this app will listen, understand, and turn your speech into text.

You can use it in different ways:
- **Automatic Mode:** Just start the app and begin speaking—your voice will be turned into text automatically.
- **Manual Mode:** Use keyboard shortcuts or a tray icon to control when the app listens, so you’re always in charge.
- **Flexible Output:** The recognized text can be placed directly into your active window, copied to your clipboard, or saved for later.

The app runs quietly in the background, ready to help whenever you need it. It adapts to your computer’s environment, works on multiple platforms, and is designed to be simple to use while providing reliable, high-quality transcriptions.
